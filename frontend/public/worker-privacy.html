Privacy Policy

Get Right Home Worker · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Get Right Home ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information of service professionals ("Workers", "you") who use the Get Right Home Worker app (the "App"). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email, profile photo, address, skills and service area.
  • Verification (KYC) details: government ID (e.g. Aadhaar/PAN/driving licence), selfie, and any certificates you upload.
  • Payment details: bank account/UPI details, earnings and payout history. Card/UPI processing is handled by our payment partner (e.g. Razorpay); we do not store full card data.
  • Location: precise location while you are online or on a job, to match you with nearby jobs, show navigation and confirm service completion.
  • Job data: bookings accepted, customer name/address/phone needed to perform the job, ratings, reviews, chat and call records with customers or support, and photos you upload as proof of work.
  • Device data: device model, OS, app version, IP address, push-notification token and crash/usage logs.

2. How we use information

  • To create and verify your account and keep the platform safe.
  • To assign jobs, enable navigation and communication with customers, and process payments and payouts.
  • To send job alerts, service messages and (with your consent) promotional notifications.
  • To investigate fraud, disputes and safety incidents, and to improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and law-enforcement requests.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information with: customers (your name, photo, rating, live location and phone/masked number during an active job); service providers acting on our behalf (cloud hosting, Firebase, Google Maps, SMS/OTP, payment processors); and authorities where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Location and permissions

The App may request location (including background location during active jobs), camera and photos (for profile and proof of work), and notifications, only where needed for these features. You can change permissions in your device settings; some features may stop working if disabled.

5. Retention

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed for legal, tax, fraud-prevention and dispute purposes (generally up to 8 years for financial records). KYC documents are deleted or anonymised when no longer required.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring to protect your data. No system is completely secure; please protect your login and OTP.

7. Your rights

You may access, correct or request deletion of your data, withdraw consent, or object to marketing, subject to law. Write to support@getrighthome.com. You may also delete your account from the App settings.

8. Children

The App is for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data of minors.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy; the latest version will always be on this page with its revised date. Continued use means acceptance.
