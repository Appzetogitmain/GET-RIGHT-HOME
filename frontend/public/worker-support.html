We aim to reply within 24 hours on working days. Urgent job-site issues are prioritised.

Common questions

I cannot log in or did not receive the OTP

Check your mobile number and network, wait 60 seconds, then request a new OTP. If it still fails, email us your registered number.

My account is pending verification

Verification usually takes 1–3 working days after you submit your ID and profile details. We will notify you in the app once approved or if more documents are needed.

I am not receiving job requests

Make sure you are set to "Online", location and notification permissions are enabled, and your service area and skills are up to date.

Payment or payout issues

Payouts are made to your verified bank account/UPI. If a payout is delayed beyond 7 working days, send us the job ID and the date of completion.

A customer cancelled or a job dispute

Report it from the job screen or email us with the job ID. We review both sides and respond within 3 working days.

Delete my account and data

Email support@getrighthome.com from your registered contact, or use Profile → Settings → Delete Account. See our Privacy Policy for retention details.

Safety concerns

If you are in danger, contact local emergency services (112 in India) first, then notify us.
